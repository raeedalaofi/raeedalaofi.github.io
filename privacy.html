This Privacy Policy explains how Valley Sun Games ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use Idle Merge Military Tycoon (also known as War Inc.: Idle Military Tycoon, package ID com.RaeedAlaofi.Idlemilitarymergebasetycoon, the "Game"). By playing the Game, you agree to the practices described below.

1 Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information when you play the Game:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, pseudonymous device identifiers, Android Advertising ID (AAID), and network information.
  • Gameplay & Leaderboard Data: Game progress, player level, highest unlocked unit, display nickname (such as your chosen Commander Name on the global leaderboard), and optional cloud save backup data stored in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Technical Logs: Crash reports, error logs, and performance data used to improve stability.
  • Advertising Data: Ad interaction data and consent signals collected by our advertising partners (Google AdMob and Unity Ads) for serving and measuring advertisements.
  • Purchase Information: If you make in-app purchases, transaction data is processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not store your payment card details.

We do not knowingly collect real names, email addresses, or other directly identifiable personal information from players unless voluntarily provided (e.g., support requests).

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game, global leaderboards, and cloud save features.
  • To serve personalized and non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob and Unity Ads mediation, subject to your consent preferences.
  • To analyze gameplay patterns and fix bugs or technical issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations (including GDPR, UK GDPR, and COPPA) and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To respond to player support requests sent via email.

3 Third-Party Services

The Game integrates the following third-party services, each operating under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP) – for in-game advertising and consent management. Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads (via AdMob Bidding Mediation) & Unity Services – for mediated advertising, in-app purchasing, and analytics. Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services – for in-app purchases and platform services. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Cloud / Firebase Firestore – for global leaderboard rankings and cloud save backups. Firebase Privacy

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these third parties, as we do not control their independent data practices.

4 Advertising, Ad ID & European Union Consent (Google UMP / IAB TCF v2.2)

We use Google AdMob (with Unity Ads bidding mediation) to display Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video advertisements in the Game. Advertising partners may use your device's Advertising ID (a resettable identifier) to deliver and measure ads.

European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom & Switzerland Users (GDPR / IAB TCF v2.2): If you access the Game from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, we present a certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) powered by the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK compliant with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2) before any advertising requests are made. Your consent choices are automatically forwarded to Google AdMob and Unity Ads.

Managing or Withdrawing Your Consent: You can review, change, or withdraw your advertising consent at any time directly inside the Game by opening the Settings / Privacy Policy button (which launches the Google UMP Privacy Options form in eligible regions), or via your device's Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization / Delete Advertising ID.

Even if you opt out of personalized advertising, you may still see contextual (non-personalized) ads. Ad revenue helps us keep the Game free to play.

5 Children's Privacy (COPPA & EU Age Requirements)

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent, such as 16 in certain European Union member states). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at raeedalaofi@gmail.com and we will promptly delete such information.

6 Data Retention

We retain collected data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this Policy or as required by law. Leaderboard records, cloud save backups, and analytics data are retained for no longer than 24 months of inactivity. You may request deletion of your leaderboard entry or cloud save data at any time by contacting us.

7 Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and strict Cloud Firestore security rules) to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8 Your Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR & Global Privacy Rights)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and California), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data (or update your Commander Name directly in-game).
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your leaderboard entry, cloud save, or personal data.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent / Opt-Out: Withdraw consent or opt out of personalised advertising at any time as described in Section 4.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 10 below.
