This Privacy Policy explains how Valley Sun Games ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use War Inc.: Idle Military Tycoon (the "Game"). By playing the Game, you agree to the practices described below.

1 Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information when you play the Game:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID), and network information.
  • Gameplay Data: Game progress, in-game activity, session duration, and feature usage statistics.
  • Technical Logs: Crash reports, error logs, and performance data used to improve stability.
  • Advertising Data: Ad interaction data collected by our third-party advertising partners for serving relevant ads.
  • Purchase Information: If you make in-app purchases, transaction data is processed by Google Play. We do not store your payment card details.

We do not knowingly collect names, email addresses, or other personally identifiable information directly from players unless voluntarily provided (e.g., support requests).

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game and its features.
  • To serve personalized and non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • To analyze gameplay patterns and fix bugs or technical issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To respond to player support requests sent via email.

3 Third-Party Services

The Game integrates the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – for in-game advertising. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services – for leaderboards, achievements, and in-app purchases. Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Analytics / Unity Ads (if applicable) – for analytics and advertising.
  • Firebase (Google) – for crash reporting and analytics. Firebase Privacy

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these third parties, as we do not control their data practices.

4 Advertising & Ad ID

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the Game. AdMob may collect and use your Advertising ID (a resettable, anonymous identifier) to deliver personalized ads based on your interests.

You can opt out of personalized ads at any time by going to your device's Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (Android). You can also reset your Advertising ID from the same menu.

Even if you opt out of personalized advertising, you will still see non-personalized ads. Ad revenue helps us keep the Game free to play.

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at raeedalaofi@gmail.com and we will promptly delete such information.

If the Game is made available in regions where a different age threshold applies (e.g., 16 in certain EU countries), we will comply with the applicable local laws.
